ABOUT ME

Since I was young, I've loved anything that feels special from drawing to crochet, something where you can see a reflection of your own soul in it. Even when I'm picking out a gift for someone, it can't just be "anything" ... it has to feel like them.

When I started learning Front end, I realized that's exactly the feeling I wanted to build into websites too: sites that actually look like the people or brands behind them, not just another generic design that could belong to anyone.

To sharpen that, I took a UI/UX design course, and I'm now learning React through the Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI, Round 5). But I don't stop at what you see on the screen. I've worked on the backend too: CineStream, a movie streaming platform with a PHP/MySQL backend, and SheMade, a bilingual e-commerce site I built end to end during my summer 2026 internship with ASP.NET Core. I love knowing how everything works behind the design, and I'm also exploring networking and security on the side.

If you want a website that actually feels like you, not just another template ... you're in the right place. And if you're a team looking for a junior developer who cares about both design and what's behind it, let's talk.
